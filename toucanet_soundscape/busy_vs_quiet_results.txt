How to read it:
  - Test 1 shows a clear difference -> the forest reacts even when the team is gone (animal reaction).
  - Only test 2 shows it, and test 3 has a positive rho -> it tracks the team's ongoing activity.
  - Groups are small; treat this as a direction, not a final answer.
